Escrow sounds like banking jargon, but the idea is simple: a neutral holder keeps the money until both sides do their part.

## The problem with direct transfer

When you send money directly to a seller, you are trusting a stranger to keep their word. If they disappear, your money is gone and recovery is slow.

## How escrow fixes it

1. Buyer pays into escrow, not the seller.
2. Seller delivers the item to the agreed campus handoff.
3. Buyer inspects and confirms.
4. Escrow releases the money to the seller.

If the item is fake, broken, or never shows, the buyer does not confirm, and the money stays safe.
