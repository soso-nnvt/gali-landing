School is expensive. A campus marketplace is one of the lowest-risk ways to make extra cash.

## Start with your room

That mini-fridge you used one session. The textbooks for a course you passed. The fan, the kettle, the chair. List them. They are inventory you already paid for.

## Then flip

Buy used items from graduating students at the end of session, clean them, photo them well, and resell at the start of the next session. The demand is predictable.

## Build a small storefront

A verified seller badge makes buyers choose you. On [Gali](https://gali.com.ng/sell), you get a storefront, escrow-backed payouts, and a campus audience that already trusts the platform.

## Keep it safe

Never hand over items before payment clears through escrow. Safe selling is repeat selling.
