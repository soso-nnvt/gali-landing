Here is the full life of a trade.

## 1. List or buy

A seller lists an item. A buyer commits to buy it.

## 2. Money is locked

The buyer pays. The funds move into escrow, not the seller's bank. The seller is notified to deliver.

## 3. Meet on campus

Both sides pick a handoff spot: faculty, library, plaza. The seller brings the item.

## 4. Inspect and confirm

The buyer checks the item. If it is good, they confirm in the app.

## 5. Payout

Escrow releases the money to the seller. Done.

## If something is wrong

The buyer does not confirm. The money stays in escrow and a dispute is opened. No silent disappearance.
