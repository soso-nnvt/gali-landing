Campus marketplaces are now normal across Nigerian universities. Here is how to use one well in 2026.

## Pick a campus-first platform

A school-focused marketplace means nearby buyers, fast handoffs, and verified users.

## Make escrow non-negotiable

If a platform cannot hold payment until you confirm, it is not safe for high-value items.

## Learn the rhythm

End of session: everyone sells. Start of session: everyone buys. List ahead of the curve.

## Protect your account

Use a school email, turn on verification, and never share your OTP.

## Start small

Your first trade should be low value, in public, in daylight. Build confidence, then scale.
