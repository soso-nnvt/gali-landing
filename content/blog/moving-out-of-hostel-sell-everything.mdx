Break is coming and your room is full of things you do not want to carry home.

## Sort in one afternoon

Keep, sell, bin. Be honest: if you have not used it this session, sell it.

## List in batches

Group similar items (kitchen, study, furniture) and post them the same day. Buyers browsing for "hostel stuff" love a one-stop list.

## Price for speed

You want these gone before travel day. Slightly lower prices beat a room full of unsold items.

## Meet at the hostel plaza

Keep handoffs on campus. Lock payment in escrow so you are not chasing transfers while packing.
