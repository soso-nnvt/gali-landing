ABUAD students accumulate a lot: course packs, calculators, kettles, mini-fridges, and phones. Selling them well puts cash back in your pocket.

## Price like a student

Buyers are students too. List at about 50 to 70 percent of what you paid, depending on condition. New-looking gadgets fetch more.

## Photos beat paragraphs

Bright, clear photos from your hostel desk beat a long description. Show flaws honestly; it builds trust and avoids disputes.

## List where students actually look

A campus-only marketplace means your buyer is nearby and can meet at the faculty or hostel plaza. That is faster and safer than posting to a national group.

## Take the money safely

Use escrow so you are never left waiting on a transfer that "is processing." On [Gali](https://app.gali.com.ng), payment locks when the buyer commits and releases when you meet.
